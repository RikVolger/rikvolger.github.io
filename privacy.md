Privacy Policy for rclone setup

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Overview
rclone is a personal, non-commercial application used by its developer to synchronize files between their own Google Drive account and their own Linux computer. It is not distributed to or intended for use by the public.

Data accessed
The app requests access to Google Drive files and folders in the developer's own Google account, solely to list, download, and upload files as part of the sync process.

How data is used
Data accessed through Google Drive is used only to perform synchronization between Google Drive and the developer's local computer. It is not used for any other purpose, including advertising, analytics, or profiling.

Storage and sharing
Files and OAuth credentials (access and refresh tokens) are stored only on the developer's own computer. No data is collected by, transmitted to, or shared with any third party or server operated by the developer. The app does not sell or transfer user data.

Google API Services User Data Policy
rclone's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and deletion
Synced files remain on the local computer until the developer deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and local credentials can be deleted by removing the tool's configuration file.
